1. EXECUTIVE ARCHITECTURE OVERVIEW & PERMISSIONS
================================================================================
The Manager role is the primary operational authority on the CST White Label platform.
A Manager oversees multiple construction projects, active sites, field supervisors,
contractors, inventory logistics, fleet operations, and financial approvals.

Key Manager Capabilities:
- Project Setup: Launch new projects, assign sites, clients, budgets, geofences.
- Approvals Gateway: Tier-1 review and clearance for payments, materials, equipment,
  and project stage milestones submitted by supervisors.
- Direct Site Execution: Log daily site diaries, direct manager expenses, and petty cash.
- Workforce Control: Register contractors, track measurements, run contractor wage reports,
  manage direct labour and daily muster rolls.
- Supply & Fleet Management: Manage raw material catalogues, equipment dispatch, and vehicle fleet.

Separation of Duties Rule:
- A Manager cannot approve requests or expenses initiated by themselves.
- Manager approvals forward requests to Organization (HQ) or issue field clearances.

4. END-TO-END BUSINESS FLOWS & STATE ENGINES
================================================================================

FLOW 1: PROJECT & SITE CREATION LIFECYCLE
  1. Manager opens `ProjectSetupWizard`.
  2. Enters site name, coordinates, category, budget, client details.
  3. Selects supervisor and milestones.
  4. On Submit (Firestore Transaction):
     - Writes to `projects`
     - Writes to `siteSupervisorMap`
     - Writes to `siteSupervisorProjectStageSchedule`
  5. Site immediately appears in active site cards and supervisor dropdowns.

FLOW 2: 4-STAGE APPROVAL PIPELINE (PAYMENTS, MATERIALS, TOOLS, STAGES)
  Status Progression:
    Stage 1: `pending_manager_review` (Submitted by Supervisor)
    Stage 2: `pending_organization_approval` (Manager verified and forwarded)
    Stage 3: `pending_manager_clearance` (HQ approved, awaiting manager final release)
    Stage 4: `approved` / `cleared` (Funds disbursed, stock released, milestone completed)
  Rejection:
    - At any point, Manager or Org can reject with mandatory `rejectionReason`.
    - Status becomes `rejected_by_manager` or `rejected_by_org`.

FLOW 3: DAILY SITE LOG & EXPENSE AUTO-SYNC
  1. Supervisor or Manager submits daily log in `ManagerSiteEntryPage`.
  2. Saves entry in `siteSupervisorEntries`.
  3. `ExpenseService.recalcTotalsAndSyncProject(siteId)` runs:
     - Aggregates materials, labour, food, fuel costs.
     - Updates cumulative spent on the parent site document in `projects`.
     - Updates `totalSiteExpensesPerDay` for dashboard financial charts.
