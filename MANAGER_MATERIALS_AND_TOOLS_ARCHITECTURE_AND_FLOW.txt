1. ARCHITECTURAL OVERVIEW & EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
================================================================================

The CST White Label (eBricks) Manager Module delivers end-to-end operational
governance over physical construction assets across two mission-critical pillars:

1. Materials Management:
   Encompasses master catalog configuration, warehouse stock warehousing,
   direct site allocations, daily on-site consumption reconciliation, and a
   rigorous 4-stage requisition approval workflow with automatic inventory
   balance updates.

2. Tools & Equipment Management:
   Encompasses asset registration (Company-Owned vs Rental), centralized
   warehouse inventory, multi-site distribution tracking, inter-site tool
   movements, site returns, and a multi-level clearance approval pipeline.

Key Architectural Tenets:
- Single Source of Truth: Inventory counts are unified through atomic database
  transactions to prevent overselling or double-allocation.
- Multi-Tenant Isolation: Every collection is tenant-scoped via
  `/organisation/{orgId}/{collectionName}` while backward compatibility is
  preserved for legacy installations.
- 4-Stage Approval Pipeline: Prevents rogue disbursements through mandatory
  role checkpoints: Supervisor Submission -> Manager Initial Verification ->
  Organization HQ Authorization -> Manager Final Clearance & Release.
- Automated Real-time Push & In-App Alerts: Every status change, stock movement,
  and clearance trigger automatically dispatches FCM push alerts and updates
  live unread badge streams.

2.3 Direct Site Material Allocation Workflow
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Source File: [lib/screens/manager/config_materialavailability.dart] (Tab 1)

Operational Behavior:
- Allows Managers to directly allocate material from Company Warehouse or Supplier
  directly to a target Construction Site.
- Form Inputs:
  * Target Site (Site ID, Site Name, Project Name)
  * Material Selection (from Master Catalog)
  * Quantity to allocate
  * Unit Rate (pre-filled from Master Price, editable by Manager)
  * Calculated Total Amount (`Quantity * Unit Rate`)
  * Allocation Date & Manager Remarks
- Execution Logic (`MaterialInventoryService.allocateMaterialToSite`):
  1. Verifies if central company stock has sufficient quantity (if deducting from warehouse).
  2. Creates an immutable allocation record in `materialAllocations`:
     `{ siteId, materialName, quantity, unitRate, amount, allocationDate, managerName }`.
  3. Atomically increments site available pool in `materialPool/{siteId}_{materialName}`.
  4. Deducts quantity from company available stock.
  5. Synchronizes mirrors in `materialsAvailability` and `materials`.
  6. Logs transaction in `materialTransactions`.
  7. Triggers real-time notification to the Site Supervisor:
     `NotificationService.notifyMaterialAllocation(...)`.

2.4 Site Material Pool & Live Consumption Monitoring
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Source File: [lib/screens/manager/config_materialavailability.dart] (Tab 2)

Operational Behavior:
- Monitors the active balance of materials residing physically across all sites.
- Metrics per Material on Site:
  * Total Allocated Quantity (Cumulative inward to site)
  * Total Consumed Quantity (Reconciled from Supervisor daily work logs)
  * Available Remaining Balance (`Total Allocated - Total Consumed`)
  * Valuation of remaining on-site material (`Available Balance * Unit Rate`)
- Reactive UI:
  Listens to Firestore snapshots on `materialPool` and `materialConsumption`,
  updating counters live as Supervisors record daily usage from the field.

Tabs & Stage Progression:
+------------------------------------+-----------------------------------------+
| Pipeline Stage                     | Actions Available to Manager            |
+------------------------------------+-----------------------------------------+
| Tab 1: 1. Mgr Review               | Initial verification of Supervisor's    |
| (status: pending_manager_review)   | request. Manager can:                   |
|                                    | a) Verify & Forward to Org HQ           |
|                                    | b) Reject with written remarks          |
+------------------------------------+-----------------------------------------+
| Tab 2: 2. Org Approval             | Informational view while Org HQ reviews |
| (status: pending_org_approval)     | the request. Displays current status.   |
+------------------------------------+-----------------------------------------+
| Tab 3: 3. Mgr Clearance            | Triggered after Org HQ authorizes.      |
| (status: pending_manager_clearance)| Manager conducts physical dispatch:     |
|                                    | a) Final Clearance & Release            |
|                                    | b) Reject if dispatch is unfeasible     |
+------------------------------------+-----------------------------------------+
| Tab 4: Approved & Released         | Completed requests. Awaiting arrival or |
| (status: approved / received)      | confirmed received by Supervisor.       |
+------------------------------------+-----------------------------------------+

Automatic Inventory Transfer on Clearance:
When Manager presses "Final Clearance & Release" (`managerFinalClearance`):
1. Verifies flag `isStockTransferred != true`.
2. Iterates each requested material in the requisition.
3. Automatically executes `MaterialInventoryService.transferCompanyToSite(...)`:
   - Decrements `companyAvailableCount` in Company warehouse.
   - Increments site inventory count in `materialPool`.
   - Records transfer in `materialTransactions`.
4. Updates requisition document:
   `isStockTransferred: true`, `status: 'approved'`, `completedAt: timestamp`.
5. Dispatches high-importance push alert to Supervisor to confirm physical arrival.

3.3 4-Stage Tools Requisition Approval Workflow
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Source Files:
- [lib/screens/manager/manager_tools_approval_screen.dart]
- [lib/services/approval_workflow_service.dart]

Operational Behavior:
- Supervisor requests tools needed for site work (e.g. Vibrator, Jackhammer).
- Follows the identical 4-stage governance pipeline:
  1. `1. Mgr Review`: Manager reviews availability in warehouse.
  2. `2. Org Approval`: HQ authorizes capital equipment mobilization.
  3. `3. Mgr Clearance`: Manager conducts physical dispatch of the tools.
  4. `Approved & Released`: Dispatched to site.

Automatic Tools Stock Movement on Final Clearance:
When Manager issues final clearance (`ApprovalWorkflowService.managerFinalClearance`):
Calls internal helper `_transferToolsCompanyToSite`:
1. Decrements available count in `toolsAtCompany/{toolCode}`.
2. Increments `toolsAtSite/{toolCode}`.
3. Updates `toolsInventory/{toolCode}.availableCountAtSites[siteId]`.
4. Creates official dispatch entry in `toolsMovement/{siteId}_{timestamp}` with
   unique Movement ID (e.g., `TM001`, `TM002`).
5. Notifies Supervisor via FCM: "🚚 Tools Requisition Approved & Dispatched!".

3.4 Tool Movement Dispatch & Return System
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Source Files:
- [lib/screens/supervisor/tools_movement_page.dart]
- [lib/screens/supervisor/supervisor_tool_movement.dart]
- [lib/screens/reports/tools_inventory_report.dart]

Movement Modes:
1. Company to Site Dispatch:
   Logged under `toolsMovement`. Managed by Warehouse & Manager clearance.
2. Site to Company Return:
   - When tools are no longer required on site, Supervisor initiates return.
   - Logged under `toolsReturn` with Return ID (e.g., `TR001`).
   - Decrements `toolsAtSite` and `toolsInventory.availableCountAtSites[siteId]`.
   - Increments `toolsAtCompany` once verified by Manager/Warehouse.
3. Inter-Site Movement:
   Transfers tools directly between two project sites, updating site allocation
   maps in `toolsInventory` atomically.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.1 Multi-Tenant Organization Scoping & Collection Resolution
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
All database accesses through `FirestoreService.getCollection(name)` resolve to:
`/organisation/{orgId}/{name}`

Where:
- `orgId` is dynamically retrieved from logged-in tenant credentials.
- Fallback routes to root collection `/{name}` if `orgId` is uninitialized.
- Ensures zero data leakage between different organizations using the white-label app.

4.4 Atomic Concurrency Controls & Firestore Transactions
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To prevent race conditions during concurrent stock updates:
1. `FirestoreService.runTransaction(...)`:
   Used in `toolsInventory` and `materialPool` balance adjustments. Reads the
   current document, checks availability, calculates the new value, and commits
   atomically.
2. `FieldValue.increment(...)`:
   Used for incrementing/decrementing warehouse stocks (`toolsAtCompany`, `toolsAtSite`)
   ensuring atomic counter arithmetic directly in the database engine.
3. Boolean Flag Write-Lock:
   `isStockTransferred: true` is checked before performing any physical stock movement.
   If another manager instance already released the stock, duplicate movements are halted.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.5 Cloud Functions & Real-Time Push Triggers
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- When a Material or Tools requisition changes state, `NotificationService`
  writes a notification record to `/notifications` and `/organisation/{orgId}/notifications`.
- Cloud Function triggers:
  * `onNotificationCreated` (root)
  * `onOrgNotificationCreated` (org-scoped)
- Functions execute `processNotificationAndSendPush()`:
  * Multicast push sent to Manager, Supervisor, and Org devices.
  * Deep-link payload points to `/manager_material_approval` or `/manager_tools_approval`.

5. END-TO-END OPERATIONAL LOGIC FLOWS (STEP-BY-STEP SEQUENCES)
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1 Flow 1: Material Master Definition & Warehouse Stock Inward
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[Manager App]
      │
      ▼ Manager navigates to Material Screen -> "Add New Material"
  1. System queries `materials` to generate next ID: `MAT005`.
  2. Manager selects Category ("Cement"), Subcategory ("OPC"), Unit ("Bags"),
     and enters Unit Rate (420.00).
  3. Form submitted -> Saved in `/organisation/{orgId}/materials/MAT005`.
      │
      ▼ Manager navigates to Material Availability -> "Company Stock" (Tab 0)
  4. Manager enters initial warehouse stock: 500 bags.
  5. `MaterialInventoryService.setCompanyStock(...)` called:
     ├── Sets `companyAvailableCount: 500` in `materialsAvailability/MAT005`.
     ├── Updates `materials/MAT005`.
     └── Logs `COMPANY_INWARD` in `materialTransactions`.
  6. Live catalog and warehouse counters reflect 500 bags available.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.2 Flow 2: Manager Direct Material Allocation to Site
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[Manager App]
      │
      ▼ Manager opens Material Availability -> "Allocate to Site" (Tab 1)
  1. Selects Target Site: "Site Alpha", Material: "Cement Grade 53".
  2. Enters Quantity: 100 bags. (Rate pre-filled: 420, Total: 42,000).
  3. Clicks "Allocate Material to Site":
      │
      ▼
[MaterialInventoryService.allocateMaterialToSite]
  4. Decrements Company Warehouse stock by 100 bags (500 -> 400).
  5. Increments Site Pool (`siteMaterialPool/SiteAlpha_Cement`):
     - `totalAllocated` += 100
     - `availableCount` += 100
  6. Writes record to `materialAllocations`.
  7. Logs transaction in `materialTransactions`.
  8. Calls `NotificationService.notifyMaterialAllocation(...)`:
     └── FCM Push sent to Site Supervisor: "📦 100 Bags of Cement Allocated to Site Alpha".

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.3 Flow 3: Supervisor Material Requisition (4-Stage Pipeline)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Stage 1: Supervisor Submission
  1. Supervisor creates request: 50 bags of Cement for Site Alpha.
  2. `ApprovalWorkflowService.submitRequest(...)` called:
     - Writes to `materialRequisition/MR_201` with `status: pending_manager_review`.
     - Appends Stage 1 audit entry.
     - Dispatches notification to Manager and Org HQ.

Stage 2: Manager Initial Verification
  3. Manager opens `ManagerMaterialApprovalScreen` -> Tab "1. Mgr Review".
  4. Manager inspects request details and clicks "Verify & Forward to Org":
     - Calls `ApprovalWorkflowService.managerVerifyAndForward(...)`.
     - Updates status to `pending_org_approval`, `currentStep: 2`.
     - Dispatches notification to Org HQ for budget authorization.

Stage 3: Organization HQ Authorization
  5. Org Admin reviews request in `OrgApprovalScreen` and clicks "Approve":
     - Calls `ApprovalWorkflowService.orgApprove(...)`.
     - Updates status to `pending_manager_clearance`, `currentStep: 3`.
     - Returns request back to Manager for physical dispatch.

Stage 4: Manager Final Clearance & Release
  6. Manager opens Tab "3. Mgr Clearance" in `ManagerMaterialApprovalScreen`.
  7. Manager verifies warehouse readiness and clicks "Issue Final Clearance & Release":
     - Calls `ApprovalWorkflowService.managerFinalClearance(...)`.
     - Automatically calls `MaterialInventoryService.transferCompanyToSite(...)`:
       * Decrements Company Warehouse: 400 -> 350.
       * Increments Site Pool: +50 bags.
       * Logs transfer in `materialTransactions`.
     - Updates requisition: `isStockTransferred: true`, `status: 'approved'`.
     - Sends FCM notification to Supervisor: "🎉 Material Released – Awaiting Arrival Confirmation".

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.4 Flow 4: Daily Material Consumption Logging & Balance Deductions
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[Supervisor App]

================================================================================
6. INTEGRITY CONSTRAINTS, AUDIT TRAILS & EDGE-CASE FAIL-SAFES
================================================================================

1. Double-Transfer Prevention:
   - When approving requisitions, the `isStockTransferred` flag is written
     atomically. Even if multiple managers click "Approve" simultaneously,
     stock is deducted only once.

2. Insufficient Stock Guards:
   - `MaterialInventoryService.transferCompanyToSite` checks that requested
     quantities do not exceed available warehouse stock before updating.

3. Complete Audit Trail:
   - Every approval step appends an immutable entry to `approvalHistory`
     containing actor name, role, exact timestamp, action, and mandatory remarks.
   - All physical movements write permanent transaction records to
     `materialTransactions` and `toolsMovement`.

4. Offline Resilience:
   - Local Firestore caching preserves allocation and requisition records on
     remote construction sites with poor cellular coverage. Once connectivity
     returns, Firestore triggers synchronize queue state automatically.
